# Reading for the code, the links and the claims

Rules: every `excerpts.`, `provenance.`, `links.`, `sources.`, `coverage.` and `facts.` rule, `sections.coverage-form`, `sections.details`, `sections.other-sources`, `sections.specifications`, `sections.documentation`, `lifecycle.figure`, `page.self-contained`, and the campaign's mandatory features and cautions.

1. The checker has settled the verbatim units, the provenance lines and the catalog's definition and usage units; do not repeat them.
2. The `regions` and `post-fence` views, then the page in slices: above every fence the prose names and links the construct and says what the excerpt is for; the sentence after it states what the lines established, with a subject the reader can identify; beside a definition every member shown is explained or grouped.
3. The `unreproduced` view: every location link to lines no excerpt shows is a lookup or a census with its total, or the lines are shown; the `reshow` view: lines reasoned about elsewhere are shown again where they are reasoned about.
4. The `claims` view: every sentence with a count or a universal word carries its scope condition; re-derive the load-bearing counts and every universal claim on a basis shaped differently from the page's (`git grep -n -w` with headers, readtags, a caller walk) and correct the wording where the tree disagrees; a restated guard keeps the code's operator and constants.
5. The `writes --fields` view for every lifecycle strip: a write in a shown unit is a mark, or the paragraph after the figure names it as left out with the reason.
6. The brief's MISSION: every backticked scope name is on the page, or named there as a seam or as left out with the reason; every helper the prose names in DETAILS has its excerpt or is named once as a seam; every hard-coded limit the walked paths carry has its value and site.
7. OTHER SOURCES: every URL is the `Link:` or `Closes:` trailer of its commit, verbatim (`git log -1 --format=%B <sha>`); SPECIFICATIONS entries are cited by a comment or commit the tree carries; DOCUMENTATION entries are files the tree holds.
8. Five symbol links by hand (`sed -n '<n>p' <path>`): the line defines the symbol or is the body line the sentence explains; every `CONFIG_*` span links to its `config` line.
9. The tests: every suite and case the scope names, and every in-tree test among the callers of a walked function, stands after the walk of the code it exercises, with what the case feeds in and expects, how it reaches the code, the option that builds it and how the tree's files say it is run; a case the page shows is a verbatim unit like any other, and the page states nowhere that a piece of code has no test.
10. The scripts and tools: every script or tool the notes name for the subject stands in the list beside the observability lists, the file linked, with what it does with the subject and how the tree says it is invoked; a test is no item of that list, and the page states nowhere that the tree has no script or tool for its subject.
