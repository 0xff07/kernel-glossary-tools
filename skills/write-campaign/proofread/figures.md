# Reading for the figures, the registers and the lifecycle drawings

Rules: every `drawing.`, `shapes.`, `geometry.`, `registers.` and `lifecycle.` rule, `arrangement.figure-placement`, the figure rows of `arrangement.block-size`, `lead-summary.lead` and `lead-summary.summary`, with the pattern index `drawing.patterns` and the figure patterns under `guidelines/page/figures/`.

1. The checker has settled the legends, the mark families, the walk paragraphs, the model figure's presence, the geometry and the ruler form; do not repeat them.
2. The outline test: the `figures` view beside the route paragraph. The titles in page order say what each subsection establishes; every function in COVERAGE has a figure carrying its concept; a stretch of subsections no title covers, or a function no figure carries, is repaired with a figure whose pattern the index gives.
3. The trigger table of `drawing.triggers` against every DETAILS subsection: a shape the subsection carries without a figure of it is drawn, unless the exempt column clears it or the subsection says why.
4. Every figure printed and judged: the strip test, the banned shapes, never a call chain, a journey or a model and never a catalog, every arrow and boundary supported by the source beside it (two checked per figure), the joins read corner by corner.
5. Section six: every register or field the DETAILS excerpts read or write is drawn, one value on one ruler, every bit range, offset and macro verified against the header that defines it (`git grep -n` the macros), the prose beside it saying what each field decides.
6. Lifecycle: every cataloged struct with a field two or more shown functions write has its strip in the subsection that introduces the object; marks in the order the writes are shown; the writers left out named after the figure.
7. The lead and SUMMARY figure limits, and every DETAILS figure within its line limit or reviewed for what it could drop.
