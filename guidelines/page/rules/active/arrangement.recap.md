# Recap

DETAILS carries a recap at the interval stated below: a paragraph opening with "So far,", within the recap target stated below, stating where the page's object stands at that point, the column of the lifecycle figure the reader has reached.

A recap stands every fourth to sixth subsection. The seventh DETAILS subsection in a row without a recap needs review; the count restarts after each recap and after each such candidate. A recap is a prose paragraph beginning `So far,`. Target 1–3 sentences, and review a longer recap rather than failing solely on its length.

Target and review boundaries:

| Unit | Measure | Target | Review candidate | Violation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a recap | sentences | 1–3 | over 3 | |


For these boundaries, P is a prose paragraph, C a source fence and D a figure fence. Count C as reproduced source lines, excluding provenance and elision lines. Count D as nonblank body lines, including legends. Apply these numerical measurements to DETAILS, including its preamble, rather than to the lead or SUMMARY.
