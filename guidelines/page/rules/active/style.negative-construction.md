# Negative construction

A sentence states what the thing is and does; the design it is not is named only where a reader would otherwise assume it.

| Pattern | Kind | Scope | Unless | Exception |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `(,\|\band)\s+(not\|never)\b\|\brather than\b\|\binstead of\b` | candidate | + figure | | a quoted comment; a comparison whose denied alternative is a real design the reader could otherwise assume |
