# Activation delta

A mechanism that can be switched on carries its activation delta, each answer from cited code:

1. what runs while it is active,
2. what code stops (say when nothing does, and put the disengaging write under the fourth question),
3. which pre-existing call sites gain a precondition, counted and cited or a representative spread with the total, after the membership test is stated in prose, and
4. what drives the return to inactive.

State the delta per active mode where modes differ and once where they do not, naming the construct that makes them identical; follow the engage and disengage paths as far as the page's subject drives them and name the symbol at which the page hands off.


When switching on and off belongs to another page's subject and this page follows one event through the mechanism, the activation-delta review may be not applicable. Name the gate and limit this page's claim to the event it actually follows; do not leave the boundary implicit.
