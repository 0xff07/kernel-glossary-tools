# Plan

Before drawing, set each box's left and right columns, each attachment and turn, and the shared columns of trunks and aligned objects. Size labels from their longest line plus padding; reuse those coordinates across rows. Count columns from 1, including the four-space indent, in terminal display cells: wide characters may occupy two cells and combining characters none.

Check uncertain glyph widths in the intended monospace rendering. A temporary ruler stays in the page's scratch directory. When a label grows, recompute the affected boxes and connectors together, including a copied reference shape. Fit an incoming arrow within the span ending at a wall or lifeline's fixed column, and keep that vertical at its planned column on subsequent rows.

A gap before it can reduce accidental column drift while editing, with spacing chosen for the drawing.
