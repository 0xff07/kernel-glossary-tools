# When

Draw a figure where a spatial, temporal, transformational or quantitative relationship would otherwise cost the reader effort to remember, recover or reconstruct: a physical layout, parallel lanes, a non-linear graph, an address space, a bit field, a ring with head and tail, two views of one structure, a quantity compared by length or height on one axis.

A relationship that prose can state still gets its figure, since the drawing is read faster and checked against the source more easily than the sentence; prose alone carries a relationship only where no pattern of the index fits it, and the subsection records why.
