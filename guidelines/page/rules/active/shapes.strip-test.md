# Strip test

Strip every label and read what is left. A figure survives when the skeleton still asserts something (this contains that, this becomes that, these ends meet here, this partitions into those, time runs this way across these actors) through a spatial property: position on an axis, a length, containment, alignment, the direction of a flow. A box is a container, not a shape; a column of boxes whose meaning is in their labels fails, and so does an axis whose positions mean nothing.
