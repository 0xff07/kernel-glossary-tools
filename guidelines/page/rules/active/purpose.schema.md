# Schema

Introduce the organization of a sequence when the reader needs it to understand the blocks that follow. Describe the stages, relationships or cases in explanation order, with the objects they act on; introduce implementing symbols where their roles become relevant. A straightforward progression may be carried by the introductions between blocks. Do not require a symbol inventory or a count of blocks in the opening: the preview earns its place by making the relationships between the parts clear.
