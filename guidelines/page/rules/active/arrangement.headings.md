# Headings

A third-level DETAILS heading is a declarative clause with a verb, within the local heading target, and true of everything in its section. Move a condition or implementation detail that the reader does not need for navigation into the opening paragraph. The fixed heading `Kconfig assumptions` of the configuration overview is the one exception, a label and no clause.

Target and review boundaries:

| Unit | Measure | Target | Review candidate | Violation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a `###` heading under DETAILS | words | up to 10 | over 10 | |


For these boundaries, P is a prose paragraph, C a source fence and D a figure fence. Count C as reproduced source lines, excluding provenance and elision lines. Count D as nonblank body lines, including legends. Apply these numerical measurements to DETAILS, including its preamble, rather than to the lead or SUMMARY.
