# Journey or model

A figure depicts a journey or a model, never a catalog: a pipeline, a sequence, a before-and-after, a lifecycle, or a structure and its relationships.

On a page organized around a journey or model the primary figure shows that spine whole, and a local figure shows the objects and boundaries of the current operation; the two may share objects, the local one keeps its orientation within the larger model, and omitted branches never turn a partial view into a claim of completeness.

Figures of successive stages keep names, positions and directions where those are unchanged and update what the operation changes.
