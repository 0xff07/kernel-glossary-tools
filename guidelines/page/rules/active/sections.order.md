# Order

Then the lead and the H2 sections listed here, in their order, SUMMARY, SPECIFICATIONS, COVERAGE, DOCUMENTATION, OTHER SOURCES and DETAILS, with the subsystem's section-six heading between OTHER SOURCES and DETAILS (REGISTERS, METHODS, PRIMITIVES or INTERFACES, omitted when the entry says none), and WEAKLY SUPPORTED CLAIMS after DETAILS when the page has an entry for it. `guidelines/page/page-template.md` shows the skeleton.

The sequence is SUMMARY, SPECIFICATIONS, COVERAGE, DOCUMENTATION, OTHER SOURCES, DETAILS. A subsystem may request exactly one REGISTERS, METHODS, PRIMITIVES or INTERFACES section between OTHER SOURCES and DETAILS; omit it when none is requested. WEAKLY SUPPORTED CLAIMS, when present, is the last section.
