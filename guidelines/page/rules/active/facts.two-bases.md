# Two bases

Every count and every universal claim holds at the documented tree, and the page states beside it the scope condition that makes it true (version, architecture, CONFIG option, file or directory), never the search: no grep, index, basis or negative search on the page, and an exhaustive member list only when the members teach a sequence, taxonomy, contrast or ownership boundary.

A claim that carries a count or a universal word is established on two differently shaped bases before it stands on the page, a symbol index against a text search over the whole tree with headers included, or a caller walk against a census, and a claim the second basis refutes is weakened to what both establish.

To check, re-derive each such claim on a basis shaped differently from the one that would most naturally have produced it, and read the sentence against the result; a count or a universal word the tree does not bear out fails.

A claim whose second basis no tool on the machine reaches is made exact instead of weak: its sentence states the scope the one check covered (the files one build compiles, one directory, one architecture), and the page's last section, WEAKLY SUPPORTED CLAIMS, names it as not cross-checked. A measurement names beside it the build it was taken on, the toolchain and the configuration.
