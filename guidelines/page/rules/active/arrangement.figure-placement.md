# Figure placement

Place a local figure where a spatial, temporal or transformational relationship would cost the reader effort to hold. Put it before or after source that needs continuous reading, never inside that source. Draw the page's model and every relationship the trigger table names, each as its own figure; a subsection that carries several shapes draws several, and the number of figures already on the page is no reason to leave one out. Vary figures with size, role and the material.
