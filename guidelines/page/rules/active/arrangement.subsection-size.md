# Subsection size

A subsection keeps the local target and review boundaries; past them it is read for a second explanatory question, a repeated conclusion or missing evidence, and split at the boundary between its parts, or kept whole when its parts cannot stand apart. Boundaries follow the explanation; no count of paragraphs or blocks bounds a subsection.

Target and review boundaries:

| Unit | Measure | Target | Review candidate | Violation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a DETAILS subsection | prose words | under 400 | over 600 | |


For these boundaries, P is a prose paragraph, C a source fence and D a figure fence. Count C as reproduced source lines, excluding provenance and elision lines. Count D as nonblank body lines, including legends. Apply these numerical measurements to DETAILS, including its preamble, rather than to the lead or SUMMARY.
