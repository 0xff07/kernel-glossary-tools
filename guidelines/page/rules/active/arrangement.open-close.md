# Open close

A subsection opens with a prose paragraph and closes with one, each of the length targets. No block directly follows a heading or a `####` heading, and no block touches the end of the subsection or another block. The prose between two blocks carries forward the object or state the first established and makes the next block's role clear.

One sentence can do that when its subject and the next construct are unambiguous; when it would have to reconstruct context the excerpts omitted, enlarge or combine the excerpts instead.

Target 2–4 sentences for an opening paragraph and 1–3 for a closing paragraph. These are reading targets. A bridge may be one sentence when it explains the previous result and introduces the next construct with a clear subject.
