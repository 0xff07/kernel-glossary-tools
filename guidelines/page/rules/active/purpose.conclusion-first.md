# Conclusion first

Open a subsection with the relationship or result it will establish, at the level the reader can understand before reading its code. Give the explanation a direction rather than an announcement of what appears below. The opening need not state the complete mechanism: introduce exact fields, branches, limits and exceptions beside the source that establishes them, keeping each condition beside the claim it qualifies.

Close with the state or relationship the subsection has established and, where useful, the remaining condition that leads to the next explanation. Repeat the opening only when the completed explanation adds meaning to it. Read the skim, every subsection's title with its first and last sentence, for a progression of objects, states and relationships; read the intervening prose and source to establish the detailed argument.
