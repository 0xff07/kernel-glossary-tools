# Block size

An excerpt and a figure keep the local reading targets: room for a stage, a short function read whole, a definition, a topology, timeline or register map. Review an excerpt past its target for whether its lines are one whole function or definition; a walkthrough piece may be shorter when its stage is shorter. Review a figure past its target for what it could drop.

A local figure may be smaller when its objects remain recognizable. These are reading targets and candidates, not automatic maximum sizes.

Target and review boundaries:

| Unit | Measure | Target | Review candidate | Violation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a `C` | reproduced lines | 6–40 | over 40 | |
| a `D` | lines | 8–80 | over 80 | |


For these boundaries, P is a prose paragraph, C a source fence and D a figure fence. Count C as reproduced source lines, excluding provenance and elision lines. Count D as nonblank body lines, including legends. Apply these numerical measurements to DETAILS, including its preamble, rather than to the lead or SUMMARY.
