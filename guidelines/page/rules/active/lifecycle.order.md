# Order

DETAILS follows the object's lifecycle: the writes a figure under DETAILS numbers are shown in the order of their marks, so that reading the subsections in order is reading the strip from left to right, and a page that departs from that order says why where it does. The model figure under the lead or in SUMMARY is an overview and binds no order, so an overview never dictates how the page is written.

The order is read in every figure under DETAILS whose legend marks belong to a figure family, a lifecycle figure or not, and in no figure above DETAILS: for each mark index in ascending order, the site of each entry of that index is first reproduced at or after the page line where the previous index's site was first reproduced, the later site among entries of one index counting; an entry whose site is later on the page than a higher mark's is a candidate, read against the departure the page states.

A legend mark outside the figure families is a candidate and gives no order.
