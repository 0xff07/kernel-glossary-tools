# Derived pages

A page derived from prior material inventories the source (catalog, sections, behaviors, enumerations, figures, references), gives every item a disposition (kept and where, merged where, or cut with the reason), shrinks the catalog and the scope statement in the same change as any cut and reports it, and passes the completeness audit on its own; every claim taken from a draft is re-found on disk.

The catalog and the scope statement define done-ness, and compression removes words, never coverage; every anchor the scope names has a location on the page or a recorded scope reduction, and removing a catalog entry never discharges it.
